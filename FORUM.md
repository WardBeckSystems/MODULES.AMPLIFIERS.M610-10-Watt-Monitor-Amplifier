>
> So let's talk WBS monitor amps. This is a good time to mention rack mount gear color (colour) - GREEN vs GRAY. About the beginning of the "golden age" I refer to, 1970's & 1980's, around the early 1970's, front panels of rack mount gear - power supplies, monitor amps, DA's, IC frames, etc - went from a light "pea green" to a more neutral light grey. About this point the M606 disappeared completely. The M610 lasted for a few more production runs, but the M621 became the mainstay of WBS amps and was continually produced for over 20 years. The M666 also had as long a run, though not in as many units as the M621. The WBS customers were primarily BROADCASTERS, unlike Neve and others who started in the RECORDING industry and later moved to broadcasting. Canadian and US broadcasters have this "thing" for plug-in modular equipment, and also in general at that time did not believe that their monitor amps needed to approach kilowatt capability. The M621 and M666 were popular with WBS customers not only because they were made by WBS, but because they were "plug-in" units. Assuming a spare was nearby, they could be changed in seconds. CBC, ABC, and CBS consumed many of these units with their consoles. And yes, I still don't know of any WBS/Bryston link regarding the M666.
>
> An "aside" on the paint color for rack mount gear, sometime at the end of the 1960's or beginning of 1970's, CBS came to WBS for their first purchase, intercom gear. CBS "sooo" didn't like the pea green color (colour) that they provided WBS with their LGBK paint for the CBS front panels. Did this have anything to do with the subsequent change to gray at WBS?
